unchanged so its behavior remains inspectable.

### Three details that can save a misleading experiment

1. **A nonzero blend weight need not change an ordering.** In V47's three-study
   visible run, the ACL branch was enabled but changed zero final values;
   effusion changed two. The receipt's `changed_targets` is the permitted target
   list; inspect `target_audit` for actual changes.
2. **Rank-fused scores are not calibrated disease probabilities.** A value of
   `0.5` is not a claim of 50% disease risk. Compare branches on the same study
   cohort and preserve target/UID alignment.
3. **A successful preview is an execution check, not validation.** Three rows
   cannot establish generalization or full hidden-test runtime. Keep
   report-related studies together when constructing your own validation split.

import hashlib, json
