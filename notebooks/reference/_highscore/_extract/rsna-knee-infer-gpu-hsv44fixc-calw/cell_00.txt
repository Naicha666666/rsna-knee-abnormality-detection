## What the ensemble sees

```text
knee DICOM series
  ├─ protocol-aware DINO transformer views
  ├─ RadImageNet medical-image views
  └─ Raptor CoAtNet study windows
       ↓
target-specific rank fusion
       ↓
12 abnormality scores per study
```

The reproduced Raptor stage in V14 uses the expanded-corpus
CoAtNet checkpoint and its diversity-residual fusion, while retaining the
transformer and radiology branches that make the ensemble less brittle than a
single architecture.
